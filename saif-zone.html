SAIF Zone MIS Platform

Management Information System (MIS) for Sharjah Airport International Free Zone — a full-stack enterprise platform handling 500+ facility services, investor portal service requests, smart approval workflows, Power Platform automation, and AI-powered production support for 5+ years.

SAIF Zone · Sharjah, UAE Jan 2021 – Jun 2026 500+ Facility Services Senior .NET Developer · Team Lead
Project Overview — STAR Format

Situation: SAIF Zone investor companies required a structured way to submit service requests and make payments for zone services — previously a fully manual, paper-driven process across 500+ service types.

Task: Design and develop an Investor Portal and internal Management Information System that automates service request routing, approval workflows, and department responses across the entire free zone.

Action: Built two integrated systems — the Investor Portal (external) and the MIS (internal). Investor requests are automatically routed to departments. Employees respond via an Online Tracking System with requirements, NOC letters, and payment receipts.

Result: 500+ facility services now automated end-to-end. Investor companies submit requests with attachments online. SAIF Zone employees initiate and complete the full service lifecycle within the MIS — zero paper required.
